What is Inverse Volatility Weighting?

Unlike traditional market-cap or equal weighting, Inverse Volatility Weighting allocates capital proportionally to the inverse of an asset's historical volatility. Assets that fluctuate heavily get smaller allocations, while steady assets receive higher weights. This constructs a risk-balanced portfolio without needing complex correlation matrices.
